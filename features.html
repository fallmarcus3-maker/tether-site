Day to day

The tools you'll touch every single day.

Time Tracking

One tap in. One tap out. Payroll's done.

Your crew clocks in from their phone and every hour lands on the right job automatically. No paper, no texts, no Friday reconstruction project.

  • One-tap punch clock tied to specific jobs
  • Shop time and site time, tracked as different things
  • Timecards export to Excel, CSV, or PDF for payroll
  • See who's on the clock right now, from anywhere
Schedule

A live view of who's on what.

A week and month view of your crew's assignments — spot gaps, move work around, and let everyone see their own schedule instead of asking you.

  • Week and month views of the whole crew
  • Assign jobs and tasks right from the calendar
  • Subscribe from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook
  • Crew sees their day in the app — no more "where am I going?"
Document Vault

Every spec, photo, and paper — findable.

The Vault files your documents by job and customer, and it's searchable down to the text inside your files. The countertop spec from March? Two seconds.

  • Filed by job and customer, not a junk drawer
  • Full-text search — including text inside documents
  • Job photos live with the job they belong to
  • Office AI can find and pull from Vault files for you
