Billing FAQ

The fine print, in plain English.

Is the app really free for my employees? ▼
Each plan includes employee seats up to the plan limit — Crew includes up to 5, Enterprise up to 10. Your crew downloads the Tether app for free; you're billed based on your plan tier. The 90-day free trial includes full access with up to 2 linked employees.
How does the 90-day free trial work? ▼
Download the app and start — no credit card required. The trial includes full access with up to 2 linked employees for 90 days. Pick a plan whenever you're ready; nothing is charged until you do.
How does billing work? ▼
Subscriptions are billed through the app store on your phone (Google Play). You subscribe, upgrade, or cancel from within the mobile app, and the app store handles the payment securely.
Can I cancel or switch plans? ▼
Yes. You can switch plans as your crew grows, and you can cancel anytime from your app store subscription settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
What if my business is bigger than 10 people? ▼
Get in touch at terry.tetherapp@gmail.com and we'll set up a custom plan sized for your crew.
